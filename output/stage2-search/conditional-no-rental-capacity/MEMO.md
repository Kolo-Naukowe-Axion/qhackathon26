# Assembly-kit reservation is not calibration-tool rental

The proposed no-rental interpretation is false for the strong final24 policies.
No new conditional bound was developed or applied.

`kit_reservation=False` disables `KitMixin`'s conceptual reservation of three
assembly-input places per live housing/rotor/flange kit. It delegates ordinary
assembly behavior. This flag does not disable investment, procurement or
calibration tools. The separate `weights.lease_calibration=True` requests the
second portable calibration kit: cost150,480-minute lead and250 investment
budget. In the full model these are minutes, not hours.

All eight24-order methods in the frozen strong-cheap final cohort preserve that
lease weight. Static methods carry it directly; the six-action and eighteen-action
learners copy it from their immutable checkpoint context. Their measured actions
change WIP/QA protection (plus T/F mode for eighteen actions), not the lease.
The final24 MPC specification also preserves leaseTrue while disabling assembly
kit reservations. The evaluator's solver-option and callback-weight paths are
distinct; neither `selected_run` nor dispatch turns kitFalse into no rental.

As a bounded saved-record confirmation, all64 predeclared retained24 traces in
`final-strong-cheap-6117508`—eight methods × eight leaves, first tape/first actor—
record exactly one calibration lease request at tick0, delivery at480 and
investment cost150. Their assembly-kit reservation flag is false. Event types
were compared exactly; substring matching would incorrectly count `release`
as `lease`. This is an event count over64 existing retained traces, not a fresh
physical audit or all2,04824-order execution event count. No outcomes were tuned.

The existing `stage2.bounds` workload/service/calendar certificate explicitly
assumes fixed initial capacity and omits leases, shocks, rework and rush work.
Its initial-kit obstruction cannot be transferred to policies that rent an
additional kit. A hypothetical no-rental policy restriction also would not
become a physical constraint on the lease-enabled model. Global optimality gaps
for the actual full model remain unknown. Older legacy learner domains are
outside this bounded claim.

`report.json` retains all64 trace paths/hashes and exact lease records, all eight
method identities, checkpoint hashes, frozen-manifest/source agreement and the
MPC specification hash. `review.py` reproduces this JSON/hash-only analysis with
the standard library. It imports no simulator, solver or checker and executes
no factory, solver, tests or external job. Frozen evidence, policies and UI are
unchanged.
