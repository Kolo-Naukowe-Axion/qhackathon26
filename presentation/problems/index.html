02 / CAUSAL FACTORY CONTROL

Choose actions under physical constraints.

The state includes every part, machine, worker, tool, buffer, robot and battery. At minute t, the policy may use public history, including disruptions already revealed.

Causal decisionat=π(o0,…,ot)

Here ot is the observation available at that time. Future quality draws and unrevealed events are excluded.

Resource capacities

Σi dir yit ≤ capr(t)

Here yit marks an occupied interval, including attendance, travel or service; dir is its demand on resource r. At every minute, overlapping actions cannot exceed any machine, crew, tool, buffer, charger or lane capacity. Travel and handling take time; outputs occupy space until pickup.

Assembly and shipping

SAo ≥ maxc∈{h,r,f} Aoc

Aoc is the physical arrival time of an accepted matching component. Assembly requires one accepted housing, rotor and flange from the same order, physically present together. The assembled pump must pass final inspection and reach shipping.

Energy balance

Bt+1 = Bt + ΔB(at)
Bmin ≤ Bt ≤ Bcap · BH ≥ Bterminal

For the stationary battery, the declared finite action table supplies ΔB, including losses. Robots also track their own charge and traction costs. Grid + solar + battery discharge = load + battery charge + curtailed power. Battery state respects losses, bounds and its terminal minimum. The grid has a time-varying capacity.

Finite quality and materials

Inspection may reveal a defect. Repair and replacement obey bounded rules and finite stock; renaming a component cannot obtain a new quality draw.

Global priorities

For a realized demand set O, cutoff H, shipment time so and due time do:

1 · Missing ordersU = Σo∈O 1{so > H}2 · Deadline debtDH = Σo∈O max(0, min(so, H) − do)3 · Model costK = grid-energy tariff cost + calibration-tool rental

Use so=∞ when an order is unshipped. Missing demand is counted first; unfinished orders contribute deadline debt only through the cutoff. Carbon is reported separately. Controller rewards are surrogates, not an exact global optimizer of this lexicographic objective.

Where the local QUBO fits

For a fixed offered set, binary xi selects a particular operation/crew assignment, with reward ri. Auxiliary bits z represent capacity slack.

E(x,z) = −Σ rixi
+ A Pconflict(x) + A Pcapacity(x,z)

Penalties encode the offered resource conflicts and capacities. The audited penalty exceeds the sum of positive rewards, so a global minimum is feasible and reward-maximizing within that pool. Approximate QAOA samples do not inherit that guarantee. Integrated simulations use at most five total bits, including slack; larger offers use a disclosed classical fallback.

The full factory is maintained classically. This is not a five-bit global factory encoding, and the default replay uses the selected classical controller.
