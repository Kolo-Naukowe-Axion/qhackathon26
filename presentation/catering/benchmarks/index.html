01 / THE PROBLEM

Choose batches and a feasible production plan.

Inputs

Meal orders and departures, recipes and batch yields, resource calendars, worker qualifications and the current kitchen state.

Decisions

Choose quantities, shared allocations, cookers, crew and preparation/cooking/packing/cleaning times on a 10-minute grid.

Constraints and events

Equipment and worker clashes, phase attendance, fresh handovers, mandatory cleaning, committed work, urgent orders, cooker outages and recovery cleaning.

Optimize in this order

Missed complete meals → discarded components → new batches → unstarted-plan changes. One lot is 10 portions. Current fixtures have R = B.

Implemented scope: paired main + side, shared batch yields, independent raw-schedule checker. Money, ingredients, holding/power capacity and interrupted cooking are outside the current model.
