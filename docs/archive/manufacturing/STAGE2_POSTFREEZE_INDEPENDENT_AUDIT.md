## Probability boundaries in reporting

A policy with zero all-orders successes on256 final tapes must not receive a population probability interval of[0,0]. The reporting-only correction suppresses that degenerate bootstrap interval and provides a conservative one-sided95% upper bound`1−0.05^wmax`, where`wmax` is the maximum normalized environment weight. For all successes, the complementary lower bound is`0.05^wmax`. The final weighted protocol gives an upper bound about1.81%, rather than an assertion of exactly zero success probability. Earlier provisional reports remain preserved and must not be mistaken for the corrected full report.

The bound was independently checked: for independent cluster outcomes`X_i∈[0,1]`, `Pr(X_i=0)≤1−E[X_i]`; using normalized weights and weighted Jensen gives `Pr(all zero)≤(1−Σw_i E[X_i])^(1/wmax)`. Inverting at0.05 yields the bound, and applying it to`1−X_i` gives the all-one case. It does not require identical distributions or Bernoulli cluster averages. It does require the stated independence/pseudorandom-tape assumption; actor replicas remain nested and create no extra independent factories. Four reporting/interval tests pass. Exact numeric checks and assumptions are retained in `final-independent-audit/boundary-probability-report.json`. This exploratory reporting correction does not expand the two primary quantum contrasts or change any policy.
