# Assembly-quality controls on the unchanged factory

`stage2/quality_policy.py` adds an optional mode-selection mixin over frozen physical recovery and process-scoped routing. It delegates every operation to the existing process implementation. Careful assembly therefore retains its longer duration, full staffing interval, energy, wear and mode-dependent first-inspection risk. Rework still uses the model's fixed residual inspection risk. No unit, repair opportunity or buffer is added.

Four controls are explicit: standard; all-careful (including repair); selective (initial pumps only in the last quarter of the horizon); original-careful (initial original-order pumps only, leaving revealed urgent orders and repairs at standard). The two selective rules require enough public remaining horizon for careful processing, known setup, optimistic mandatory QA/transport and a60minute margin. This is a ranking guard, not a completion guarantee. The rule never reads quality uniforms, seeds, unrevealed urgent orders or hidden shock outcomes. Original-careful uses the order's publicly revealed urgent flag. The timing threshold is shared across all orders, with no keyed unit-specific decisions.

Earlier retained negative evidence matters: `output/stage2-search/process-modes-train8/summary.json` reports n12 all-careful assembly105/108 shipments and6/8 complete cases versus108/108 and8/8 for standard. A slower quality mode is not a free improvement.

The first new experiment ran27 deliberately selected TRAIN episodes, comparing standard/all-careful/last-quarter selective under identical controls within each context. All27 were checked valid; wall time25.71s on two local workers. Sources, declared tasks, all traces, terminal states and QA results are retained under `output/stage2-search/assembly-quality-targeted-training/`. These selected failures do not estimate unbiased completion probabilities. On three selected n13 failures with WIP6/guard4/routing-off, standard shipped45/45, selective44/45 and all-careful42/45. On four selected n14 failures with WIP6/guard4/QA routing, standard shipped60/64 and the slower controls each57/64. Neither slower rule is promoted on that evidence.

For n12/seed15 under the old WIP8 control, last-quarter selective changes P11's actual initial assembly to careful, lowers its first-inspection risk and ships it: total fulfillment rises12/14→13/14, while U01 remains unfinished. Under the stronger WIP6+QA-routing control, P11 reaches assembly much earlier and selective does not trigger; both standard and selective ship13/14. All-careful's changed chronology can increase wear enough that P11 still fails its first inspection and fixed-residual repair. Thus even legal careful assembly is not a universal rescue. Diagnostic QA uniforms are post-run evidence only and are never inputs to the policy.

A bounded nine-episode follow-up tests original-careful on precisely these same known TRAIN cases. All nine passed the checker in7.43s wall time. This also regresses against the stronger standard controls: n13 ships44/45 and n14 ships58/64; n12 WIP6 stays13/14 but tardiness rises4616→9055ticks. It is not promoted. The protocol is `output/stage2-search/assembly-quality-original-train-tasks.json`; all sources, traces and outcomes are under `output/stage2-search/assembly-quality-original-training/`.

No validation or reserved final tapes are executed by this driver. The CLI accepts only TRAIN0–31, freezes source hashes, refuses output overwrite and retains invalid episodes. Tests check exact default physical equivalence, mode choice from public data, full careful attendance, actual independent physical checks and paired causality. Source guards are checked before and after each episode. The three new careful heuristics remain rejected development controls, rather than selected finalists.
