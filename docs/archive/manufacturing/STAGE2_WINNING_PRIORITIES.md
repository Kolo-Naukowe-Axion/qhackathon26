## Why presentation matters

The [official challenge](../../../Quantum_Hackathon_2026_ERP_Challenge.pdf), §11,
allocates 30 points:

| Area | Available points |
|---|---:|
| QUBO formulation, solution quality, extension creativity/complexity | 15 |
| Development Log, Project Design, consistency between log/code/presentation | 9 |
| Demo video and live presentation/Q&A | 6 |

Thus half the available points concern documentation, explanation and defense.
They are not 15 points for visual styling. Section5 explicitly credits a polished
engineering/presentation layer when it showcases a strong extension. A clear
interactive Gantt and end-to-end execution fit this purpose.

The visual priority is a short route through the actual result: show capacity
loss, the late plan, recovery, the original pool's loss, enrichment and the
quantum/classical comparison. Keep labels readable, the result provenance clear,
and the live action reliable. The existing visual system can support this;
a fresh aesthetic direction is not a required deliverable.
