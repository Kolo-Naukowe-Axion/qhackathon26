# Guarded strong static controls

`stage2/static_control.py` declares one bounded factorial before comparison with richer learned controllers. Its purpose is to prevent a learned-policy claim from merely rediscovering a better fixed WIP, speed mode or completion heuristic. It uses the existing full96-hour minute-grid factory, hard tool homes, physical kit recovery and family admission. No reward override, dead-slot reclaim, physical-capacity change or final tape is included. The maintenance-outage scheduling guard is explicitly on for every variant; earlier unguarded invalid maintenance traces remain retained separately.

The grid has288 policies: n12/n16 each have72, and n24 has144. Controls are WIP4/6/8 ×QA protection0/4 ×machining eco/standard/turbo ×QA-only ETA routing off/on ×completion credit0/270minutes. n24 additionally toggles the previously defined moderate quarter-strength join priority. Other weights are fixed within each context. Kit reservations stay on for n12/n16 and off for n24, matching their existing strong contexts. Precision restrictions and all mode-dependent physical durations, crew requirements and quality probabilities remain in force.

Every policy is declared on the same TRAIN0–31 environmental tapes:9216 episodes. Execution is split into two independent32-CPU Slurm array parts, n12+n16 and n24, each with a30-minute limit and a1740-second driver budget. No GPU is requested. Each part first completes every policy on tapes0–15. A measured, conservative halfway forecast starts tapes16–31 only if they fit. If a later cap interrupts that second phase, candidate selection uses only the completed balanced first16; every extra raw row remains retained, and any observed invalid disqualifies its policy even outside the balanced selection scope. Incomplete first phases cannot select candidates.

Selection is lexicographic: fewest unfulfilled orders, then aggregate deadline debt, then total cost, then stable policy ID. Every context retains at most its top three distinct eligible policies and the exact original prior with the same guard enabled. A fixed prior that has an observed invalid remains visible but is ineligible. First-tape traces for every policy and every invalid trace are saved; every actual episode receives the full independent physical checker and selected/committed equality check. Source hashes are checked before and after every episode.

`stage2/static_control_validation.py` prepares only declared TRAIN-selected candidates and each original prior on explicitly reused VALIDATION100–115. Before any grid validation, the third-candidate rule was prospectively clarified: use global TRAIN top2 plus the best matched18-action static policy (QA routing off, completion credit0, BOMoff), then the original prior, deduplicated. The matched policy's global TRAIN rank and domain are recorded. This replaces the third global candidate without increasing the maximum four policies/context or validation budget. It supplies a direct comparator to a dynamic learner whose actions are WIP4/6/8 ×QA protection0/4 ×machining eco/standard/turbo. The earlier v16p1 validation-source snapshot retains the initial top3 rule and was not executed; v16p2 captures this clarified rule.

Validation verifies the exact TRAIN source hashes, all16 paired tapes per candidate and no more than four distinct candidates per context. It rejects altered policies, missing tapes, guard changes, TRAIN tapes and reserved final tapes. Known TRAIN invalids remain disqualifying. This is selection on reused validation, not held-out final evidence.
