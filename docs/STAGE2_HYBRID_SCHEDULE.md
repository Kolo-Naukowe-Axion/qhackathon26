# Learned time schedule with corrected local selection

`stage2/hybrid_schedule.py` composes the frozen selected CEM6 controller with corrected no-batch local rewards and the causal maintenance-outage guard. It retains the same full physical recovery, hard tool homes, kit-reservation choices, family admission, route choices and mode constraints as the selected checkpoint. This is a learned time-dependent policy with local quantum/classical optimization. The selected CEM controller uses eight fixed720-minute segments; it is not a state-adaptive RL policy, although the unchanged factory decoder reacts to the observed state.

The exact checkpoint is `output/stage2-search/final-preparation/checkpoints/strong-cem6-2026.json`, SHA256 `d2ae8337daadd1baec7840e29b69500d91a548bbd3c0b4b5378f2b22ba6203e9`. Loading verifies its bytes, every original source hash, action domain and selected schedule shapes. The selector is overridden in the controller's base weights before construction, so subsequent callbacks preserve that selector rather than silently reverting to priority. Actor seeds are independent of environmental seeds. Guard true and no-batch reward are explicit fixed parts of this integration.

The unchanged no-batch reward is200 per offered operation plus up to100 from normalized public priority. Every original candidate ID, fixed crew assignment, machine/tool conflict and cumulative power constraint remains present. It removes the earlier100-per-additional-batch-unit bonus; it does not make a local optimum globally optimal or enlarge the candidate crew domain. Priority remains a sequential decoder control and makes no QUBO calls. QAOA still has its explicit five-total-bit simulator limit; larger pools use disclosed classical fallback, not hidden quantum execution.

One264-episode TRAIN0–7 pilot uses n12/n16/n24 contexts. Priority and exact run once at actor7; SA, QAOA and equal-shot uniform controls each use actors7/13/29. Actors are nested replicas within the same environmental tape. All methods share the frozen outer schedule, context, reward and candidate-construction rules. Their later realized pools can differ after different actions; they are not falsely labeled identical-pool continuations. Every actual pool digest, selected/committed IDs, actual circuit/coupling/fallback/repair counts, bit limits, physical and independent lineage checks, all failures and33seed0 traces are retained. Timing uses literal recorded elapsed seconds; nested angle-optimization time is not added a second time.

The experiment is a bounded integration test, not assumed quantum advantage or a new policy search. No validation or reserved final tape is included. Pretraining is not free: the retained CEM artifact reports9096 fit episodes,720 reused-validation episodes,1216.09s wall time and32 workers, plus its shared strong-prior preparation costs referenced in the checkpoint. Those costs belong alongside the new inference-pilot accounting; they are not replaced by the pilot's shorter runtime. The immutable v19 sources, exact checkpoint, submission and results are under `output/stage2-search/axion-hybrid-schedule-v19-submission/` (Slurm6117500).

Four tests cover exact checkpoint rejection, the264-run/33-trace domain, canonical physical trace equality to the direct audited priority/exact composition, persistence of selector/actor settings through every callback, three actual paired-causal checked traces and separation of coupled simulation, trivial one-bit calls, classical fallback and decoder mismatch. No physical source is edited.
